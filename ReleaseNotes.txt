Release notes for the orLibrary
===============================
The orLibrary is an extension framework and collection of opt-in extensions, 
used to enhance Inform 6's Standard library.  Based on versions produced in 
2001-2004, this second major revision was introduced as a public beta in 2026.

Version 2.0b (Jan 2026)
------------------------
This is the first public beta for this second major release of the orLibrary.
It contains 48 "Primary" extensions targeted at general-purpose projects, plus
an additional 21 "Supporting" extensions which provide low-level features 
which may serve as a foundation for extension developers to build upon.

More detail about this releases contents, the "Primary" extensions in 
particular, can be found in the orLibrary User's Guide (orLUG), which accompanys
the library.  Additionally, this free, online viewer will let you view it 
without committing to the install:

https://nbviewer.org/github/onyxring/orLibraryI6/blob/main/src/_extras/orLUG.pdf

A special thanks goes out to @otistdog, for his review of the library and the 
documentation and suggestions.  His attention to detail is exceptional.
